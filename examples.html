example 04 · covert chess

shown An unedited session of the live demo. A move is chosen and a prompt entered; Agent A emits the message; Agent B recovers the move from the text alone — one symbol out of the legal moves available in that position — and replies with its own move embedded the same way. Neither agent is told the move.
