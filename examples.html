what is bam

Choosing, not altering

Every token a model samples is a choice among many continuations it considers roughly equally good. BAM — Burnashev Adaptive Posterior Matching — treats that choice as a communication channel. The sender steers which of the likely continuations gets sampled; the receiver, reading only the finished output, refines a belief about what was meant. Nothing is edited after the fact, so what ships is still an honest sample from the model's own distribution.

The two sides both watch the same output as it appears, so the sender always knows what the receiver currently believes and can spend the next token on whatever is still in doubt. That feedback is what makes the scheme reliable on short, ordinary turns — and it needs no shared weights, no shared prompt and no shared cover statistics.
