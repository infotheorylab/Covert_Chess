how to read the diagnosis

What the receiver actually does

The receiver never sees the prompt, the weights or the cover distribution — only the emitted tokens. Each token multiplies its posterior over the candidate set (communication phase). When the posterior crosses γ₁ = 0.5 the decoder stops accumulating, fixes a candidate and asks the sender to confirm it (confirmation phase, shaded green in the chart). An ACK closes the round; a NACK knocks the candidate down and returns to the communication phase — which is why the blue curve sometimes collapses and climbs again. That feedback loop is what buys the order-of-magnitude error reduction over fixed-budget watermarking.
